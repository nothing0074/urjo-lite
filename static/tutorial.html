Play the game Rules Create
How to play: every line (row) needs equal blue (X) and red (O) spots. Neighbouring lines must differ. A number on a spot means exactly that many of its up-to-8 neighbours share its colour. Tap a spot with the selected color; tap a filled spot to clear it.
